How We Work

Simple 4-Step Process

From initial site consultation to flawless final steel erection.

01

Discuss & Survey

We visit your site in Bengaluru to take precise dimensions, review drawings, and understand load requirement parameters.

02

Design & Planning

Material selection (MS/SS grades, section sizes), CAD blueprint verification, material cost estimate, and scheduling.

03

Fabrication & Finish

Heavy cutting, precision TIG/MIG welding, weld bead grinding, red oxide anti-rust priming, and powder coating.

04

Delivery & Erection

Safe transport to site, anchor bolting/field welding assembly, final alignment check, and client handover.
